## 1. Business model

FLAMINGO is an online marketplace connecting Indian employers with independent professionals for shift-based engagements. FLAMINGO does not employ the professionals. Engagements are limited to four days per week, with shifts of 8, 10 or 12 hours.

## 2. The reference transaction

| Step | Amount |

5. Invoice series, mandatory fields, and the e-invoicing threshold for our turnover.
6. Any requirement to issue a self-invoice or a payment voucher.

**Direct tax**

9. Who is responsible for deposit and return filing where the professional is an individual vs a company vs a partnership?

**Classification**
11. On a four-day-per-week, fixed-shift roster with platform-set rates, is the professional an independent contractor? Identify the factors that would create an employer-employee relationship and how to avoid them in the contract.
12. Any Shops & Establishments, professional tax or labour registration triggered in [state(s)].
